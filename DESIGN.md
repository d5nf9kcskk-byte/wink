# Design System: Wink

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Tri-Band Paperback"**

Wink is set like a 1935 paperback cover: a flat genre-coloured band on top, a white title band in the middle, a genre band at the bottom. The reader's current book fills Home as that cover, and its bottom band is the Start reading control. Everything else in the app is built from the same material: bands of flat ink, white title bands, numbered spines on a shelf, and a small price-line register for facts ("Print · p. 312 of 736 · about 16 sessions left"). There is one typeface, a humanist sans in the Johnston/Gill line, used in bold mixed case for titles and spaced caps inside bands.

The density is low and the scale is large. Controls are whole bands, tall enough for a thumb, not buttons floating on cards. Objects sit on a cool shelf grey so the white title band reads as paper and the cover reads as an object. The world is flat: no gradients, no drop shadows, no cream paper. Depth comes from colour blocks, from a quiet tone-on-tone endpaper texture inside each genre band, and from motion. The cover hinges open on its spine into a two-page spread, its leaves turn, bands fill left to right, numbers roll, and spines slide into their slots.

Motion follows one grammar and has three settings (Full, Reduced, Off). Celebration grows with significance: a finished session fills a band, a level-up wipes a band across the Reader No. line, and a finished book floods the whole screen in the book's ink.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Tri-band composition: genre band, white title band, genre band.
- Every book paints in its genre's ink through six generic properties (band, band-on, band-deep, band-deep-on, band-accent, band-pattern).
- One humanist sans (Cabin Variable), tabular numerals throughout.
- Flat colour, 3px corners at most, no drop shadows; a tone-on-tone endpaper texture is the one permitted surface pattern.
- Bands are the controls, at least 48px tall and 88px for the hero.
- Motion scales with significance and obeys Full / Reduced / Off.

## Colors

The palette is near-black ink on a cool grey shelf, with white title bands, plus nine soft pastel genre inks that do all of the colour work.

### Primary
- **Genre Inks** (`g-*` family, 8 genres plus Unclassified Grey): the book's own colour, now a pastel family (previously fully-saturated hues). Used for the top and bottom cover bands, spines, the goal band fill, the level-up band, week spines and year-shelf spines that were read, and the full-screen flood when a book is finished. Each ink carries five parts: the pastel itself, an `on` text colour (a near-black `#1f1d24`, distinct from body ink `#1a1a1a`), a `deep` shade for pressed states and fills, a `deep-on` text colour, and an `accent` — a saturated pull of the same hue used only for thin rules (the cover/title-page divider, the week-spine and year-shelf-spine border). Every ink/on and deep/deep-on pair passes WCAG AA (4.5:1), verified by `src/lib/genres.test.ts`. In dark mode each pastel and its deep shade and accent step down to a slightly richer version of themselves (`g-<genre>-night` / `-deep-night` / `-accent-night`) so bands still read against the dimmed paper; `on`/`deep-on` stay fixed. **Status: shipped but PENDING THE OWNER'S APPROVAL.** Treat the hues as provisional. The five-part structure (ink / on / deep / deep-on / accent) and the AA floor are the durable rules.
  - Fiction Peach, Mystery Sage, Thriller Brick, Romance Pink, Fantasy Lilac, Biography Periwinkle, Nonfiction Sky, Young Readers Butter, Unclassified Grey.
- **Endpaper Pattern** (`g-<genre>-pattern`, one per genre): a small tone-on-tone SVG repeat drawn in the book's own ink at 5-10% opacity — diagonal strokes, dots, a herringbone, an arc, a star, a grid, a wave, sparkles, a faint hairline — layered under the flat genre colour wherever a genre band paints (the cover's top/bottom bands, the book-thumbnail placeholder, week spines, year-shelf spines, the inside of the book-opening cover). It never appears alone; it is always `background: var(--band-pattern, none), var(--band)`.

### Neutral
- **Press Black** (ink): all body text, the outline on outlined steppers, the Reader No. fill, the open-session banner and the week-spine shelf rule. Also the fallback band on sheets that have no book.
- **Graphite** (ink-2) and **Pencil** (ink-3): secondary facts and hints, the missed-day spine outline, input strokes.
- **Title White** (paper): the white title band, sheets, the open session page and the cover page. In dark mode it dims to **Bedtime Grey** (paper-night) so the cover isn't a flashlight at night.
- **Shelf Grey** (ground) and **Shelf Shadow** (ground-2): the app background, empty goal bands, future week spines and disabled bands.
- **Hairline** (rule): nav borders and the empty Reader No. track.
- **Correction Red** (danger): input errors only.
- **Night set** (`*-night`): dark mode swaps the ink and ground for light ink on a near-black shelf. Genre pastels deepen slightly rather than staying identical (see Genre Inks above); the `on`/`deep-on` near-black text stays fixed in both themes.

### Named Rules
**The Book Paints Itself Rule.** Colour in any component comes from the current book's genre through the band, band-on, band-deep, band-deep-on, band-accent and band-pattern properties (`genreVars()`). No component hard-codes a genre hue.

**The Always-Paper Rule.** A surface that is always white (the cover body, sheets, the session page, the welcome cover) carries `.on-paper`, which pins light-theme ink in both themes. Dark mode dims the paper; it never inverts it.

**The Pressed Goes Deep Rule.** A band's active state swaps to its deep ink and deep-on text. When the band is already deep (the Finish save band), pressing takes it to Press Black.

**The Accent Is A Line, Not A Fill Rule.** `band-accent` (a saturated pull of the genre hue) only ever draws a thin rule or a 1px inset border — the cover/title-page divider, the week-spine and year-shelf-spine outline. It never fills a surface; fills stay on band/band-deep.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Cabin Variable (with Gill Sans, Gill Sans MT, Trebuchet MS)
**Body Font:** Cabin Variable, the same family
**Label/Mono Font:** none distinct; numbers use tabular figures set globally

**Character:** One humanist sans in the Johnston/Gill tradition, the face of the paperback it imitates. Weight and case do the work: bold mixed case for titles, spaced caps for band labels.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (700, clamp 2rem to 3.25rem, 1.02): book titles on the cover, the book-opening title page, the Finish band and the page-number input. Balanced wrap, clamped to three or four lines.
- **Timer** (500, clamp 4.5rem to 7.5rem, 1): the session clock only.
- **Headline** (700, 1.375rem, 1.15): section headings such as the streak line, the page-entry question and coming-soon titles.
- **Band** (700, 1.25rem, 1, +0.01em): the label on the hero band (Start reading), the goal band on desktop and the level-up band.
- **Label** (600, 1rem, 1.25): the author, band labels at standard height, the banner and errors.
- **Body** (400, 1rem, 1.5): guide copy, capped at about 34 to 40ch.
- **Fact** (500, 0.875rem, 1.35): the price-line register: format · page · sessions left, hints and nav tab labels.
- **Caps** (600, 0.8125rem, 1, +0.14em, uppercase): text set inside a band, such as the genre name, Reader No., sheet titles, the Finished stamp and the week-day letter.
- **Spine** (600, 0.6875rem, 1.3, +0.08em, uppercase, vertical): spine titles and spine numbers.

### Named Rules
**The Caps Live In Bands Rule.** Spaced caps belong to band text (genre, Reader No., sheet titles, spines, the Finished stamp). They never sit above a heading as a label or kicker.

**The Price Line Rule.** Facts go on one line in the fact register, separated by middle dots, with tabular figures: "Print · p. 312 of 736 · about 16 sessions left".

## Layout

The layout is mobile first and built on a 4px base (4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64). There is more space above a heading than below it. On phones, Home stacks top to bottom: a horizontally scrolling strip of spines (other current books, hidden when there is only one), then the cover filling the remaining height to the fold (`max(360px, 100svh - 264px)`), then the goal band, seven week spines and the Reader No. line. The Start band sits in the thumb zone. On phones shorter than 700px, the cover thumbnail is dropped to keep Start in the first viewport. The Streak screen composes the year as four quarter-shelves of week-spines, unchanged from this same tri-band material.

Navigation is a bottom tab bar on phones and becomes an 88px left rail at 720px. From 960px, the cover keeps paperback proportions (11:18) on the left and progress sits on the right. At that width the goal band grows to full band height (88px). Sheets rise from the bottom on phones and become a centred 480px leaf from 720px. Every tap target is at least 48px. Screens handle their own safe-area insets, and the shell only clears its own bar, rail and banner.

Breakpoints: 720px (rail, centred sheets), 960px (cover beside progress).

## Elevation & Depth

The system is flat and has no drop shadows. (The `--lift` shadow token was removed during review.) Depth comes from colour blocks against the shelf grey, from the white band against genre ink, from the quiet tone-on-tone endpaper pattern under a genre fill, and from motion: the cover opens on its spine in 3D perspective and its leaves turn, and sheets and spines slide in. The only box-shadows are inset state marks drawn in the element's own text colour: a 3px ring on a selected band, a 5px underline on the next PIN cell, and a 1px inset border in band-accent on read spines. All three mark state or a genre edge. None lifts anything.

A 50% near-black scrim (`rgb(10 11 13 / 0.5)`) sits behind sheets.

### Named Rules
**The Flat Stack Rule.** Surfaces never cast shadows. To separate one layer from another, use a colour block, a 1 to 2px rule, a scrim, or the tone-on-tone endpaper pattern. Inset rings in currentColor are allowed only to mark a state such as selected or next.

## Shapes

Corners are nearly square, like a trimmed paperback. The book radius (3px) is the maximum anywhere: on the cover, spines, sheet leaves, inputs and outlined steppers. Bands that run full width (Start, goal band, standard band, page-entry alternatives) have no radius at all (0). Week spines and year-shelf spines are rounded only at the top and stand on a 2px ink shelf line. Borders are 1px for inputs, quiet outlines and read-spine accents, 2px for steppers, PIN cells and the add spine, and 3px for focus and selection. The cover's rule under the title, and the book-opening title page's rule under its title, are a 40×2px bar in the book's accent ink.

## Components

### Bands (the primary control)
Whole-width flat bars in the book's ink. You press the band itself; there is no button sitting on a card.
- **Shape:** square (0), full width, 48px minimum; hero bands are 88px.
- **Default:** band / band-on, Label type (Band type on the hero).
- **Active:** swaps to band-deep / band-deep-on at 160ms ease-out.
- **Selected:** a 3px inset ring in band-on.
- **Focus:** a 3px outline in band-on, inset by 7px.
- **Disabled:** Shelf Shadow with Pencil text.
- **Progress rule:** an 8px strip along the top edge, filled in band-deep by scaleX with exponential ease-out (900ms at Full).

### The Cover (signature)
The tri-band paperback: a genre band (genre and Reader No. in caps, drawn over its pastel's endpaper texture), then a white title band (a 56×84 thumbnail, the Display title, a 40px accent-ink rule, the author, and the fact line), then the hero Start band. The two genre bands split the spare height equally. Radius 3px, clipped.
- **Opening (the signature interaction):** on Start, the cover plays a book-opening sequence (~1.7s at Full): the cover swings open on its left spine (perspective 3D, weighted ease so the board starts slow, swings, and settles) to reveal a two-page spread lined with the genre's endpaper pattern inside the covers; four leaves turn in a staggered sequence over the title page, each face carrying a greeked-text impression (abstract, unequal-length ink bars at 16% opacity — never legible words) so the pages read as printed rather than blank; the view then zooms into the title page, which grows to fill the screen and becomes the white session page. Reduced fades straight to the session page; Off skips straight there. A tap or Escape/Enter/Space during the sequence skips ahead.
- **Swap:** changing books fades and slides the cover in (24px, 320ms; no slide unless motion is Full).

### Spines
Book spines in genre ink (with its endpaper texture), 52px wide minimum and 144px tall, with a vertical caps title and a number at the foot. Long titles wrap into more columns, so the spine gets thicker like a fatter book. Active goes deep. The selected spine gets a 3px band-on border. The Add spine is a 2px Press Black outline.

### Week Spines / Year Shelves
Seven equal columns on a 2px ink shelf line per week, 7:16 aspect, rounded at the top only. A read spine carries its genre's flat colour plus endpaper pattern and a 1px band-accent inset border. Other states: grace (2px dashed ink), missed (1px Pencil outline), pending (2px ink outline), future (Shelf Shadow). A newly earned spine rises 24px into its slot (only at Full). The Streak screen repeats this unit across four quarter-shelves to read the whole year; the composition and states are identical to Home's week strip.

### Goal Band
A square Shelf Shadow bar. The fill is a clipped copy of the label in band / band-on, so text stays legible on both sides of the fill edge. Clip-path grows over 900ms with ease-out.

### Reader No. Line
Label name, a 4px Hairline track (6px on desktop) filled in Press Black, and a fact line. **Level-up:** a band in the book's ink wipes across the line, holds the new number, then recedes. Below Full motion it fades instead.

### Sheets
A white leaf with a head band (56px) in Press Black, or in the book's ink for genre sheets. Caps title and a 48px close button. The leaf has a 3px top radius on phones and 3px all round when centred. It enters with a 560ms slide.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** a 1px Pencil stroke, 3px radius, white fill, 48px minimum height. The page number is set in Display type with tabular figures.
- **Steppers:** 48px squares with a 1 to 2px ink outline. Active inverts to ink.
- **Error:** the border and message turn Correction Red.
- **Focus:** the global 3px ink outline at 2px offset.

### Navigation
Tabs use the Fact type in Graphite on the shelf. The active tab turns Press Black at weight 700 with a 3px ink bar on its outer edge. The layout is a bottom bar on phones and an 88px rail with the Wink mark from 720px. The open-session banner is a 48px Press Black band docked above the bar.

### Finish (the largest celebration)
The whole screen floods with the book's ink. A white title band holds the title and a caps Finished stamp. The title rises 40px as it fades in, and the rating follows in sequence. The save band uses the deep ink, and pressing it goes to Press Black.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** paint components through band / band-on / band-deep / band-deep-on / band-accent / band-pattern from `genreVars()`, never a literal genre hex.
- **Do** keep every genre ink/on and deep/deep-on pair at 4.5:1 or better and extend `genres.test.ts` when adding an ink. The current hues are pending owner approval.
- **Do** keep `band-accent` to thin rules and 1px borders only; never use it as a fill.
- **Do** keep the endpaper pattern quiet (5-10% ink opacity) and always layered under the flat band colour, never used alone.
- **Do** put `.on-paper` on any surface that stays white in both themes.
- **Do** make the band itself the control: full width, at least 48px, 88px for the primary action.
- **Do** time every CSS transition with `--dur-1..4` (160 / 320 / 560 / 900ms) and `--ease-out`, and every Motion animation with `dur(pref, seconds)` and `EASE_OUT`, so Full / Reduced / Off governs all motion.
- **Do** scale celebration with significance: a session fills a band, a level-up wipes a band, a finished book floods the screen.
- **Do** set facts in the price-line register with tabular figures.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use drop shadows or gradients. Inset currentColor rings for selected or next state, and the endpaper texture, are the only departures from flat colour.
- **Don't** round corners past 3px, and don't round full-width bands at all.
- **Don't** use cream or warm paper. Title White is pure white, dimmed to Bedtime Grey (#dedfe0) at night.
- **Don't** build dashboards of cover-thumbnail cards, stat tiles or progress rings. Progress is a band fill, a spine or a rule.
- **Don't** set caps outside a band, or as a kicker above a heading.
- **Don't** add a second typeface.
